    Lines & chemistry

    Friends +6Same circle +2Chemistry is counted for whoever is on the ice together, including the goalie. Each group starts at 40.

    Open a team to edit it. Pick a player in any slot to move them there; whoever held the slot swaps into their old spot, and the roster sheet updates to match. Power-play and penalty-kill slots can stay on Auto (dashed) or be set by hand. Forward lines and defense pairs rotate on their own, so line 1 often skates with pair 2.

    Roster sheet

    Teams
    CSV columns: name, short, no, team, pos, line, style, sht, pas, hnd, skt, spd, def, chk, fo, sta, dis, clu, goal, friends, personality, personality2, cares1, cares2. Older files with a single off column still import. Separate friends with semicolons. Importing CSV replaces players; JSON replaces teams and players.

    Positions: C, LW, RW, D, G. Lines 1–3 dress a skater on that forward line or defense pair; goalies are Starter or Backup; Extra sits a player. Shooting and Passing replace the old Offense rating. Hands sets how well a player dekes, dangles, saucers passes and protects the puck. Skating is acceleration, edges and balance; Speed is top speed. Checking wins hits and board battles, Faceoffs wins draws, Stamina sets how fast a player tires and recovers, Discipline sets how often they take penalties, and Clutch lifts (or sinks) a player late in close games, in overtime and in shootouts. Hover a column header for details. Stats run 1–99 with Uma-style letter grades. Changes save automatically and apply from the next game.
